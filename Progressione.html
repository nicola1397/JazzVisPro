Scale Calculator Notation

The calculator parses chord progressions and suggests appropriate scales. It understands the following formats:

  • Chords: Standard notation like Cmaj7, G7, Am7, F#m7b5. Flats (Db) and sharps (C#) are both accepted.
  • Separators: Use spaces, newlines, or pipe symbols (|) to separate chords.
  • Alterations: Specify alterations in parentheses, e.g., G7(b9, #5). This will often suggest the Altered Scale.
  • Repeat Signs: Use •/• or % to repeat the previous measure's chords.
  • Minor Chords: Use m, -, or min for minor. E.g., Dm7, D-7.
  • Diminished: Use dim or ° for diminished. E.g., Ddim7, D°7.
